Privacy notice

This page covers the signup website only. It is written to be read, not to protect us. If anything here is unclear, call us and ask.

Last updated: 2026

What we save

When you fill in the contact step, we save:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and phone number, whichever you gave us
  • Your ZIP code
  • Whether you told us we may call or text you
  • Whether you were signing up for yourself or for someone you care for
  • Which event or link brought you here
  • The date and time

We save this as soon as you complete that step — before you answer anything else, and whether or not you finish. We do that on purpose, so that being interrupted does not mean starting over. We tell you before you type anything, which is why this is written on the first page too.

What we do not save

The questions about health conditions, insurance, doctors, and your home are answered on your own phone or computer. Those answers are never sent to us. They are held in your browser's memory while you use the page and are gone when you close it.

When you reach the last page, the only thing added to your record is a yes or no: whether the answers pointed toward being a fit, and whether our clinical team should call. Not which conditions you picked, not your insurance, not anything about your bedroom.

We want to be straight with you about the limit of that: a record saying “this person may be a fit for home health monitoring” still says something about you, even with no details attached. It is much less than a health questionnaire. It is not nothing.

Who can see it

SwitzerHealth staff working on signups, and our clinical team. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

What you send goes directly into our own Microsoft 365 systems, which we use under an agreement with Microsoft that covers health information. The pages of this website are delivered by Netlify, our website provider — but what you type does not pass through them. It goes from your browser to Microsoft.

Calls and texts

We only call or text you if you ticked the box saying we may. That box is never ticked for you, and you can finish the questions without it. If you change your mind, tell us on the phone or by email and we will stop.

Tracking

There is no advertising tracking on this website. No analytics, no pixels, no third-party cookies. The site stores a short reference number in your own browser so that your answers survive a reload.

One other thing is kept in your browser, and only when something goes wrong: if your connection drops as you send your details, they are held on your own device and sent again the next time the page loads. That copy is deleted once it goes through. If you filled this in on a device that is not yours, closing the browser or clearing its data removes it.

Having your information deleted

Email care@switzerhealth.com or call 385-340-3130 and ask. You do not need a reason. If you have your reference number from the last page it is faster, but we can find you without it.

Once you enroll

Everything above is about this signup website. If you go on to enroll in monitoring, your health records are handled under HIPAA by our clinical practice, with the separate notice of privacy practices you will be given at that point.
